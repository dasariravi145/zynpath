⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTICE CONCERNING SUBSCRIPTIONS

Deleting your Zynpath account permanently removes your game data and cloud progress. However, account deletion does NOT automatically cancel recurring subscriptions managed through Google Play. You must cancel any active subscription directly in your Google Play Account Subscriptions to avoid continued renewal charges.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Account Identity: Cloud Player ID, Public Zynpath ID, and linked Google/Facebook authentication tokens.
  • Profile & Progression: Display name, equipped cosmetic themes, avatar frames, level completion history, and cloud stars.
  • Social Graph: Friend relationships, pending invitations, block lists, and notification preferences.
  • Active Sessions: All issued authorization tokens and real-time WebSocket connections are immediately revoked.

Legitimate Retention Exceptions

  • Competitive Match History: Completed multiplayer match records retain an anonymized entry displayed as [Deleted Player] to maintain leaderboard mathematical integrity.
  • Financial Audit Logs: Cryptographic hashes of past Google Play purchase tokens are retained in anonymized form solely to prevent fraudulent re-licensing and satisfy statutory tax/accounting requirements.

Submit Deletion Request Without App Reinstallation

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policy, you can initiate account deletion directly via this web page without reinstalling the mobile application.
