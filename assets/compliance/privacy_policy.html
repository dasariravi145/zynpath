Privacy Policy

Zynpath: Number Path Puzzle • Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Guest-First • Offline-First • Zero Data Sale

1. Introduction

Welcome to Zynpath: Number Path Puzzle ("Zynpath", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a clear, honest, and transparent experience.

Our Core Promise: Zynpath is engineered on a Guest-First, Offline-First architecture. You can play 100% of our Solo campaign levels (300 handcrafted puzzles across 6 worlds) and solve the Daily Challenge completely offline without creating an account, without entering an email address, and without providing any personal details.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, how it is secured, and your rights to manage or delete your data.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device (Offline-First)

When you play Zynpath as a Guest or offline, all gameplay progression resides strictly in sandboxed local storage on your Android device (using Android Room and DataStore):

  • Solo Level Progression: Unlocked worlds, completed level stars (1–3), solve durations, moves count, and personal completion records.
  • Daily Challenge History: Daily completion records and consecutive streak counts.
  • Player Settings: Sound volume, haptic feedback toggle, theme selection, and reduced motion accessibility preferences.

This local data is not transmitted off your device unless you voluntarily link a cloud account.

2.2 Data Collected When You Voluntarily Link an Account (Optional)

If you choose to link a cloud identity (Google Sign-In or Facebook Login) to enable cloud backup or online multiplayer:

  • Authentication Identifiers: A unique subject identifier provided by Google or Facebook to verify your session. We never receive or store your third-party account password.
  • Public Profile Data: Your self-chosen display name, equipped avatar frame, and randomly assigned Public Zynpath ID (e.g. ZYN-8492-ECHO).
  • Cloud Progression: Level completion stars and streak records synchronized across devices.

2.3 Real-Time Online Multiplayer Data

When you participate in Quick Duel, Friend Duel, or Mini League matches:

  • Match Records: Start and finish timestamps, elapsed solve duration, and placement (1st–5th) recorded for competitive rankings.
  • Social Graph: Mutual friends list, pending invitations, and your block list.
  • Preset-Only Interactions: In-match communication is strictly bounded to preset positive emojis and phrases (e.g., "Well played!", "Good match!"). We do not operate or store open free-text or audio chat.

2.4 Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

Purchases for optional Premium subscriptions or puzzle packs are processed exclusively via Google Play Billing. We store a cryptographic SHA-256 hash of the Google Play purchase token and entitlement status to unlock ad-free play and cosmetic themes. We never receive, process, or store credit card numbers or financial account details.

2.5 Optional Rewarded Advertising

Free players who exhaust their standard free hint allowance may voluntarily choose to watch a rewarded video ad via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to receive bonus Solo hints. Ads are 100% optional, capped at 5 per day, and completely disabled for Premium subscribers.

2.6 Data Categories We NEVER Collect

Data Category Collection Status Architectural Guarantee
Location Data NEVER COLLECTED Zero coarse or fine GPS permissions in manifest.
Contacts / Address Book NEVER COLLECTED Friend discovery operates strictly via Public Zynpath IDs; address book is never accessed.
Camera / Microphone NEVER COLLECTED No photo taking, video recording, or audio streaming permissions.
Free-Text Messages / Audio Chat NEVER COLLECTED Zero free-form text input; strictly preset emojis.

3. How We Use Information

We process information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  1. To deliver core puzzle gameplay and preserve your progress.
  2. To enable live multiplayer matchmaking, synchronized puzzle countdowns, and fair-play validation.
  3. To verify entitlements and restore purchases via Google Play Billing.
  4. To prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse through authoritative server-side move verification.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information or device identifiers to third-party data brokers.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted platform services that adhere to stringent privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing: App distribution, system updates, and in-app purchase verification (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Optional rewarded video ads for free Solo hints (Google Ads Privacy).
  • Google Identity & Facebook Login: Optional account authentication tokens.

5. Data Security Safeguards

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the app and our servers uses Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS / WSS). Cleartext HTTP is disabled in production.
  • Hardware Keystore Storage: Session tokens on Android are encrypted with hardware-backed keys via the AndroidKeyStore (AES/GCM/NoPadding) and excluded from device backups.
  • Rate Limiting & Anti-Abuse: Server endpoints enforce sliding-window rate limits and deny-by-default access tiers.

6. Account Deletion & Data Rights

6.1 In-App Self-Service Deletion

You can permanently delete your cloud account at any time within the Android application:

Open Settings → scroll to Data Management → tap "Delete Account" → confirm the prompt.

6.2 External Web Deletion (Without Reinstalling App)

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy, you can also delete your account via our official web portal without reinstalling the app:

Zynpath Account Deletion Portal (or visit https://zynpath.com/delete-account when configured).

IMPORTANT NOTICE ON SUBSCRIPTIONS:
Deleting your Zynpath account does NOT automatically cancel recurring subscriptions managed through Google Play. You must cancel active subscriptions directly in the Google Play Subscriptions Center to avoid future renewal charges.

6.3 Data Retention & Purge Scope

  • Immediately Purged: Cloud Player ID, public profile, linked OAuth credentials, friends list, pending invitations, and active sessions.
  • Local Progress: Reset independently on your device via Settings → Reset Local Guest Progress.
  • Legitimate Retention Exceptions: Completed multiplayer match records retain an anonymized participant entry ([Deleted Player]) to maintain competitive leaderboard integrity. Cryptographic purchase token hashes are retained solely to satisfy legal, tax, and anti-fraud audit obligations.

7. Target Audience & Children's Privacy

Zynpath is a general-audience logic puzzle game designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to us, please contact us at privacy@zynpath.com so we can promptly delete it.
