# Zynpath: Premium Analytics & Progression Insights Specification

## 1. Overview
Zynpath Premium provides passionate players with deeper personal analysis and performance progression tools under the entitlement feature key `ADVANCED_PERSONAL_STATS`.

In accordance with Zynpath's Fair Monetization and Zero Fabrication policies:
1. Premium analytics never provide competitive advantages (no puzzle hints in competitive matches, no altered timing rules).
2. Premium analytics never invent or simulate player data.
3. Essential statistics remain 100% free and permanent for all players.

---

## 2. Unlocked Capabilities

### 2.1 Completion Trends & Velocity
- Chronological bar and line visualizations showing puzzles solved per day.
- Breakdown of first-time completions versus replay practice sessions.
- Filtering by Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, and All Time.

### 2.2 Same-Puzzle Time Improvements
- Deep analysis of personal best improvements on specific puzzle layouts.
- Time delta and percentage improvement calculated strictly across identical puzzle versions.

### 2.3 Per-World Progression Analytics
- Detailed breakdown of solve speed across Worlds 1–6.
- Comparison of average solve times across increasing difficulty tiers (4x4, 5x5, 6x6 grids).

### 2.4 Premium Solo Pack Analytics
- Dedicated progress tracking for downloaded Premium Solo level packs.
- Completion percentages, pack-specific best times, and star totals isolated from the 300 free levels.

---

## 3. Subscription Lifecycle & Expiration Policy

### 3.1 Subscription Expiration
- **Record Preservation:** All underlying gameplay records (solve times, completion timestamps, replays) are preserved indefinitely in local Room persistence.
- **Access Gating:** The "Premium Insights" tab switches from the detailed chart view to the standard Premium Preview Card.
- **Core Stats Unaffected:** All free statistics (completed levels, world progress, daily streaks, basic match history) remain fully visible.

### 3.2 Resubscription
- When a subscription is renewed or reactivated, access to all advanced charts and trend analysis is restored immediately.
- Players are never required to replay completed levels to regenerate their historical statistics.

---

## 4. Free User Preview Experience
For players on the free tier:
- The Premium Insights tab displays an honest, transparent preview of available capabilities.
- The preview does not fabricate sample numbers or simulate player progress.
- Explanatory copy clearly describes the metrics that will be calculated once unlocked.
- Includes a direct action button to view subscription tiers in the Premium screen.
