# Google Play Store Screenshot Plan & Specifications — Zynpath

## 1. Sequence & Strategic Objectives

The screenshot sequence guides potential players through the unique gameplay loop, volume of content, competitive excitement, and progression:

```
[Screen 1: Home Screen] ──> [Screen 2: Solo Gameplay] ──> [Screen 3: World Progression] ──> [Screen 4: Daily Challenge]
          │
          ▼
[Screen 5: Quick Duel 1v1] ──> [Screen 6: Friend Duel] ──> [Screen 7: Mini League] ──> [Screen 8: Achievements & Profile]
```

## 2. Detailed Screenshot Specifications

### Screenshot 1: Home Screen
- **Headline Banner:** "ONE PATH. EVERY NUMBER."
- **Content:** The main Home screen showing brand title, session continuity hero ("Continue Level 14"), mode selection cards, and sync badge.
- **Goal:** Immediately establish the core puzzle premise and show clean, modern navigation.

### Screenshot 2: Solo Puzzle Gameplay
- **Headline Banner:** "CONNECT CHECKPOINTS IN SEQUENCE"
- **Content:** Live active board with a continuous glowing cyan path originating from Checkpoint 1, passing Checkpoint 2, routing around crimson walls, and aiming for final Checkpoint 4.
- **Goal:** Teach the primary game rule visually: single path, ascending numbers, wall corridors, 100% cell coverage.

### Screenshot 3: World Progression
- **Headline Banner:** "6 WORLDS • 300 OFFLINE LEVELS"
- **Content:** Interactive World Selection map highlighting World 1 (Awakening), World 2 (Binary Drift), and World 3 (Labyrinth of Walls) with level counts and star ratings.
- **Goal:** Prove substantial, high-value single-player progression playable 100% offline.

### Screenshot 4: Daily Challenge
- **Headline Banner:** "GLOBAL DAILY CHALLENGE"
- **Content:** Daily Challenge screen showcasing today's unique 6x6 board, countdown timer to UTC midnight reset, and consecutive day streak counter.
- **Goal:** Highlight daily retention and global community participation.

### Screenshot 5: Quick Duel 1v1
- **Headline Banner:** "REAL-TIME 1v1 DUELS"
- **Content:** Quick Duel active race with split-screen progress indicators, opponent progress HUD, live reaction pills, and timer.
- **Goal:** Showcase fast-paced competitive puzzle racing on identical boards.

### Screenshot 6: Friend Duel
- **Headline Banner:** "PRIVATE FRIEND MATCHES"
- **Content:** Friend Duel room lobby with 6-character room code, ready confirmation, and rematch trigger.
- **Goal:** Demonstrate private social play with friends.

### Screenshot 7: Mini League
- **Headline Banner:** "2–5 PLAYER MINI LEAGUES"
- **Content:** Multiplayer party room showing 3 participants, live coverage percentages, and 45-second finishing countdown.
- **Goal:** Appeal to group and tournament play.

### Screenshot 8: Achievements & Profile
- **Headline Banner:** "MASTERY MILESTONES & STATS"
- **Content:** Achievement Gallery showing category tabs (Solo, World, Mastery, Daily, Competitive), unlocked badges, and personal records.
- **Goal:** Demonstrate depth of personal mastery and completionist rewards.

## 3. Production Capture Guidelines & Privacy Protections

- **Genuine Capture Requirement:** All screenshots must be captured from actual running builds in Android Studio emulator or physical test devices. Never substitute invented UI mockups or non-existent mechanics.
- **Synthetic Test Identities:** Screenshots must use synthetic demo names (e.g. `PathMaster`, `ZenithSolver`, `AlphaRunner`) and test Zynpath IDs (e.g. `ZYN-1001-DEMO`).
- **Zero PII Exposure:** Never display real email addresses, backend auth tokens, private IP addresses, or real friend invites.
- **Game Rule Strictness:** Ensure paths shown in screenshots obey all rules: orthogonal moves only, ascending checkpoint order, no diagonal cuts, no wall crossings.

## 4. UI Consistency & Asset Validation (Prompt 48)
- **Visual Parity**: Evaluated actual Compose UI screens against the 8 screenshot specifications. Confirmed exact match in typography, color tokens, wall styling, checkpoint disks, and HUD alignment.
- **Promotional Alignment**: Verified that promotional captions match real, verified gameplay (orthogonal moves, dual-win criteria, 6 worlds, 300 offline levels, real-time duels). No deceptive or simulated mechanics exist in the store asset plan.
